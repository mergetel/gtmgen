Why this exists

    
I spent years in enterprise software watching strategy decks get made. Every one had a 2x2 with the company alone in the top right, and nobody ever asked who picked the axes. It was the company.

    
The 90-day plan was always the best part. Twelve workstreams, and the only one that did anything was telling customers what shipped. That's the one I kept skipping. Merging was fine. Saying it out loud was the hard part.

    
I use merge.tel every day to promote merge.tel and my other projects, this one included. It turns merged pull requests into posts and customer updates, so the telling happens whether I feel brave that day or not.

    
This site invents a framework every Monday and merges it, so it gets posts too.
